LLM (Large Ling Model)

What's happening?

Built from a dissection of 53 real Ling Lab papers (2004–2026). Each draw writes a complete paper: title, abstract, introduction, methods, results with simulated observers and real statistics, three to eight figures in the lab's style, a parameter table, discussion and references.

Here is the uncomfortable part. Twenty-two years of my lab's papers reduce to a script of a few hundred kilobytes. It knows our questions, our stimuli, our methods, our figure style and the shape of our arguments, and it will write another paper every time you press the button. That is a little terrifying.

It is also a release. If the papers compress this easily, they were never the whole of the work. The work lives where the bytes don't reach: the questions that kept us up, the experiments that failed before one worked, the people who came through the lab and left as scientists. The papers are a record of that. Maybe I can stop treating them as the gold-standard currency of a scientific life.
